## Who We Are

Founded in 2020, we set out to create a shopping experience that puts quality
and craftsmanship first. What started as a small curated collection has grown
into a destination for thoughtfully designed products.

## Our Mission

We believe that everyday objects should be both beautiful and functional. Every
product in our collection is chosen for its quality, design, and the story
behind it.

## Our Team

We're a small, passionate team based in Berlin. We work closely with designers,
artisans, and independent brands to bring you products that stand the test of
time.
