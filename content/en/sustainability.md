## Our Commitment

Sustainability isn't a trend for us — it's a responsibility. We're committed to
reducing our environmental impact at every step.

## What We Do

### Responsible Sourcing

We partner with suppliers who share our values. All our partners meet strict
environmental and ethical standards.

### Packaging

We use recycled and recyclable packaging materials. Our shipping boxes are made
from 100% recycled cardboard, and we've eliminated plastic from our packaging.

### Carbon Offset

We offset the carbon emissions from every shipment through certified climate
projects.

## Our Goals

- **2025:** 100% of packaging from recycled materials
- **2026:** Carbon neutral operations
- **2027:** Full supply chain transparency
